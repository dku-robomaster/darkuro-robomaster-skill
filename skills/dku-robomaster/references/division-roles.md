# Division roles

Source: DKU RoboMaster – Position Descriptions, one-page team recruitment PDF supplied in the project. This is a baseline role description, not a current equipment list.

## Mechanics
Design and optimize physical robot structures, chassis, gimbals and launching/feed mechanisms; use CAD, manufacturing, 3D printing, assembly and testing. Coordinate packaging, loads, mounting and wiring space with other divisions.

## Electronic Control
Develop STM32 embedded systems using C/C++; integrate motors, sensors, communication and control loops; implement and tune robot motion. Circuit design is an optional development direction. Coordinate command/feedback interfaces with Mechanics and Computer Vision.

## Computer Vision
Develop target detection, tracking and aiming perception using Python/C++, image processing and appropriate vision libraries. The recruitment document mentions OpenCV, YOLO and NVIDIA Jetson as learning examples; it does not establish a mandatory stack or purchased hardware. Coordinate target estimates, timing and interfaces with embedded control.

## Integration
Agree on coordinate frames, units, timestamps, command meanings, feedback, update rates, mechanical mounts and failure/stop behavior. Verify the complete chain with measurable tests.
